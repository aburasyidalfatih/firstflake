# Public Recreational Gold Panning – Eastern & Midwest States (Beginner Research)

Compiled 2026-10-06 from official agency pages where available (USFS, state DNR/geological survey/state parks), with secondary sources flagged. Fees and permit rules change; always call the managing office before you go. "Pay-to-pan" sites are private businesses listed because beginners can legally pan there without land-status homework.

---

## North Carolina

- **Uwharrie National Forest – Uwharrie River & tributaries** (Troy / Eldorado, Montgomery Co.) · USDA Forest Service, Uwharrie Ranger District · Pan + shovel free, no permit; hand sluice/"shaker box" tolerated; no motorized gear, no digging banks, no wilderness or W&S river corridors; metal detecting not allowed · https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/northcarolina/permits/recreational-mineral-collection (county summary: https://www.montgomerycountync.gov/visit/what-to-do/pan-for-gold-in-the-uwharries)
- **Reed Gold Mine State Historic Site** (Midland, Cabarrus Co.) · NC Dept. of Natural & Cultural Resources · Pay-to-pan, about $4/pan of real site ore, Apr 1–Oct 31, 9–4; site admission free · https://events.dncr.nc.gov/reed-gold-mine
- **Thermal City Gold Mine** (Union Mills, near Marion) · Private · ~$10/person/day pan or sluice native placer; trommel/highbanker rentals; camping; mid-Mar–Oct · http://www.huntforgold.com/
- **Lucky Strike Gold & Gem Mine** (Vein Mountain, Marion) · Private · River panning/own sluice for campers free, day panning small fee, sluice rental ~$10; year-round · http://luckystrikegoldandgem.com/

Rules: No state permit for hand panning; rules depend on landowner. National forests allow pan/shovel without permit; no motorized or suction equipment without USFS authorization. State parks: no mineral removal at all. Most gold-belt land is private — get written permission.
Check first: https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/northcarolina/permits/recreational-mineral-collection

## Georgia

- **Chattahoochee National Forest streams (Dahlonega, Chestatee, Etowah, Tesnatee drainages)** (Dahlonega / Helen) · USDA Forest Service, Chattahoochee-Oconee NF · Pan + small hand trowel only, in streambed only; no sluice, no dredge, no permit or fee; wilderness, scenic river corridors and some trout streams closed; confirm mineral ownership with district ranger · https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/chattahoochee-oconee/about-area/faqs
- **Consolidated Gold Mine** (Dahlonega) · Private · Underground tour + panning lesson, paid; open daily · https://www.dahlonega.org/things-to-do/gold-mining/
- **Crisson Gold Mine** (Dahlonega) · Private · Pay-to-pan by the pan or bucket; trommel use free with 6 buckets; open daily · https://crissongoldmine.com/
- **Dukes Creek Gold & Ruby Mines** (Sautee Nacoochee, near Helen) · Private · Gold panning buckets ~$15; year-round · https://exploregeorgia.org/sautee-nacoochee/outdoors-nature/gemgold-mining/dukes-creek-gold-and-ruby-mines

Rules: No state permit for hand panning; EPD has a recreational mining exemption for non-mechanized panning (https://epd.georgia.gov/recreational-mining-exemption). National forest: pan and trowel only, streambed only, no sluices/dredges. State parks (incl. Smithgall Woods / Dukes Creek) prohibit panning. Private land needs permission.
Check first: https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/chattahoochee-oconee/about-area/faqs

## South Carolina

- **Sumter National Forest – Long Cane & Andrew Pickens Ranger Districts** (Edgefield / Abbeville / Walhalla) · USDA Forest Service · Historically allowed with a FREE letter of authorization from the district office (pan + shovel only; no sluice or dredge; map of closed areas supplied). A 2026 forum report says Long Cane staff now say panning is not allowed — call to confirm (Long Cane RD 803-637-5396) · https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/francismarionsumter

No verified free public site currently confirmed open; no pay-to-pan sites found in SC. Nearest beginner pay-to-pan options are in NC (Reed, Thermal City) or GA (Dahlonega).

Rules: SCDHEC does not regulate recreational panning or small dredges; mining permit needed only for bank disturbance/excavation. State parks and WMAs prohibit collecting. Sumter NF requires written authorization; mechanized gear not allowed. Most gold ground is private — get written permission.
Check first: https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/francismarionsumter

## Virginia

- **Lake Anna State Park – Goodwin Gold Mine tour & Gold Panning on the Beach** (Spotsylvania) · Virginia State Parks (DCR) · Ranger-led programs, Memorial Day–Labor Day Wed–Sun plus some weekends; beach panning free, mine tour/pan $3 pre-registered; park parking fee applies; no self-guided panning · https://www.dcr.virginia.gov/state-parks/lake-anna
- **Gold Mining Camp Museum at Monroe Park** (Goldvein, Fauquier Co.) · Fauquier County Parks & Rec · Free daily panning demos Mar–Nov 2 pm; paid sluice with purchased gem/gold bag (cash only); Wed–Sun · https://www.fauquiercounty.gov/government/departments-h-z/parks-and-recreation/facilities/parks/monroe-park
- **George Washington & Jefferson National Forests** (Roanoke / Harrisonburg; little placer gold) · USDA Forest Service · Hobby panning allowed without permit for personal use; no mechanized gear; metal detecting restricted · https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/gwj/forest-products/recreation-mineral-collecting

Rules: No state permit for hand panning; regulated by Virginia Energy and DEQ general environmental law. The Piedmont gold-pyrite belt is mostly private — written permission required. Contrary Creek (Louisa Co.) is often cited but access is from road right-of-way only; not an official site.
Check first: https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/gwj/forest-products/recreation-mineral-collecting

## Alabama

- **Talladega National Forest – Shoal Creek & Talladega Ranger Districts** (Heflin / Talladega; Talladega Creek area) · USDA Forest Service · Hand panning permitted; GPAA and local sources say a free permit/letter from Shoal Creek Ranger Station (256-463-2272) is required and no sluice, highbanker or dredge · https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/alabama/offices/shoal-creek-ranger-district
- **Alabama Gold Camp** (Lineville, Clay Co.) · Private · Daily per-person fee for pan/sluice/detecting, extra for highbanker/dredge (≤5 in); RV and cabin camping; gear rental · https://alabamagoldcamp.com/
- **GPAA Alabama claims (Cornhouse Creek, Curvins Cow Pasture)** (Clay Co. area) · GPAA members only · Membership required · https://www.goldprospectors.org/

Rules: No state permit for hand panning on navigable public waters; mechanized gear may need state/federal permits. State parks (ADCNR) do not allow mineral extraction. Talladega NF: confirm permit requirement with the ranger district. Most gold ground (Hog Mountain, Arbacoochee) is private.
Check first: https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/alabama

## Vermont

- **Camp Plymouth State Park – Buffalo Brook** (Plymouth / Ludlow) · Vermont Forests, Parks & Recreation · Hand panning only, east of park entrance; no sluice, rocker, or motorized gear; day-use fee · https://vtstateparks.com/plymouth.html
- **Rock River** (Newfane / South Newfane / Dover) · Mixed private and town land; listed by Vermont Geological Survey · Hand pans; landowner permission needed off public ground · https://dec.vermont.gov/sites/dec/files/geo/StatewidePubs/rockhound3.pdf
- **Williams River** (Ludlow / Chester, along VT-103) · Mixed private; VGS-listed · Hand pans; landowner permission · same VGS PDF
- **Mad River** (Warren / Waitsfield / Moretown) · Mixed private; VGS-listed · Hand pans; landowner permission · same VGS PDF

Rules: No state permit for hand panning with minimal disturbance. FP&R lands: traditional hand panning only. Sluice boxes allowed only on private land with landowner permission AND a DEC Rivers Program mineral prospecting permit (802-828-1115). Green Mountain NF: hand tools as casual use; check Manchester/Rochester RD.
Check first: https://dec.vermont.gov/sites/dec/files/geo/StatewidePubs/rockhound3.pdf

## New Hampshire

- **Wild Ammonoosuc River & Tunnel Brook – White Mountain National Forest** (Woodstock / Easton, Pemigewasset RD) · USDA Forest Service · FREE annual permit from any WMNF district office required; gold pan and small hand trowel only; no shovels, sluices, rockers, or dredges · https://www.fs.usda.gov/r09/whitemountain/recreation/opportunities/other
- **Wild Ammonoosuc River at Swiftwater Covered Bridge** (Bath, Rte 112) · Town-owned bridge access; adjacent riverbed private · Hand pans; landowner permission for riverbed; no shovel digging · https://www.des.nh.gov/sites/g/files/ehbemt341/files/documents/2020-01/geo-1.pdf
- **Twin River Campground** (Bath) · Private campground catering to panners · Pan on-site for guests · secondary source only (treasurenet) — verify

Rules: Riverbeds are privately owned by abutters — written permission needed outside the National Forest. Hand panning needs no state permit; no shovel digging in bed or banks. Sluice or suction dredge requires NHDES Wetlands Bureau permit ($25 resident/$50 non-resident, expires Dec 31). State parks: no collecting.
Check first: https://www.des.nh.gov/sites/g/files/ehbemt341/files/documents/2020-01/geo-1.pdf

## Maine

- **Swift River at Coos Canyon** (Byron, Rte 17) · State rest area; riverbed private but panning customary · Hand pans, no bank disturbance; free; Coos Canyon Rock & Gift across road rents gear and gives free lessons May–mid-Oct (207-364-4900) · https://www.maine.gov/dacf/mgs/explore/minerals/facts/goldfact.htm
- **East Branch Swift River** (Byron) · Private timberland (historically Boise Cascade); panning with timber manager permission; work below water line only · secondary (MGS lists stream; access status dated)
- **White Mountain National Forest – Maine side** (Bethel area) · USDA Forest Service · Free WMNF permit required; pan and hand trowel only · https://www.fs.usda.gov/r09/whitemountain/recreation/opportunities/other

Rules: No state permit for hand panning if banks are not disturbed (38 M.R.S. §480-Q(5)); streambed is private — get permission. Motorized prospecting (≤7 hp) only Jun 15–Sep 15 and only in streams ≥4 ft wide, with some streams closed; Unorganized Territory follows LUPC Ch.10 §10.27,G.
Check first: https://www.maine.gov/dacf/mgs/explore/minerals/facts/goldfact.htm

## Pennsylvania

- **State Forest streams (statewide, e.g. Clearfield Co. – Moose Creek / Little Clearfield Creek; McKean Co. – Potato Creek)** · PA DCNR Bureau of Forestry · Recreational non-commercial hand panning permitted in State Forest rivers/streams except Wild & Natural Areas and EV/HQ waters; no bank/channel disturbance; no permit · https://www.dcnr.pa.gov/StateForests
- **York County streams (Bermudian, Conewago, Codorus, Muddy Creek tributaries)** (Dillsburg / Castle Fin) · Mostly private · Hand pans with landowner permission · secondary source (raregoldnuggets.com) — not an official site

No verified named free public site; the DCNR authorization is forest-wide rather than site-specific. No pay-to-pan gold sites found in PA.

Rules: 1961 State Forest Lands Prospecting Act allows casual prospecting on State Forest land; State Parks require written DCNR permission; State Game Lands — no removal. DEP: no permit for non-motorized pans/sluices; suction dredges/highbankers need a DEP permit. Avoid HQ/EV streams.
Check first: https://www.dcnr.pa.gov/StateForests

## Maryland

No verified free public site; no pay-to-pan options found in Maryland. The gold belt around Great Falls lies in C&O Canal NHP, where panning is prohibited; all state parks (Patapsco, Seneca Creek, etc.) are closed to collecting without Maryland Park Service authorization. Nearest beginner options: Monroe Park or Lake Anna in Virginia.

- **Gold Mine Loop Trail (Maryland Mine ruins)** (Potomac, C&O Canal NHP) · National Park Service · Hiking/history only — NO panning · https://www.nps.gov/choh/

Rules: Panning only legal on private land with owner permission, hand tools; state lands closed without Forest & Park Service permission; federal park land needs superintendent approval (not granted recreationally).
Check first: https://www.mgs.md.gov/geology/minerals_energy_resources/gold.html

## Tennessee

- **Lyons Creek & Wildcat Creek – Cherokee National Forest, Tellico RD** (Tellico Plains / Coker Creek) · USDA Forest Service · Only designated Class 1 hand-panning streams; pan and hand tools, no mechanized gear; no permit fee; stream maps from Tellico RD (423-253-8400) · https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/r08/cherokee/publication/2025%20JH%20DRAFT_Gold%20Collecting-Fact%20Sheet.pdf
- **Coker Creek Gold Camp** (Coker Creek, Hwy 68) · Private · Pay-to-pan on private frontage of Coker Creek; call (850) 354-2279 for hours · https://www.visitmonroetn.com/gold-panning
- **Coker Creek Welcome Center** (Coker Creek) · Monroe County tourism · Info, maps, and (reported) permit registration for NF panning · https://www.visitmonroetn.com/gold-panning

Rules: No state permit for hand panning. Cherokee NF allows hand panning only in designated streams; Coker Creek itself is closed on NF land (sediment-impaired, mercury in sediments) — private property only. Class 2 (mechanized) prospecting is not permitted anywhere in the forest.
Check first: https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/cherokee/recreation/coker-creek

## Michigan

- **State forest / state game area streams where the State owns surface AND mineral rights** (e.g. Allegan State Game Area, Yankee Springs Rec Area, Green Creek – Muskegon Co.; reported by WOOD-TV, not DNR) · Michigan DNR · Hand panning year-round, no permit; ≤15.55 g (½ troy oz) gold per person per year; no digging banks; closed on designated trout streams, Natural Rivers, Great Lakes bottomlands, natural areas, mussel beds · https://www.michigan.gov/dnr/managing-resources/minerals/recreational-gold-panning-and-sluicing-on-state-land
- **Barkus Campground** (Pewamo, Ionia Co.) · Private · ~$10/prospector to pan Stony Creek (price from 2023 article; verify) · secondary source (Genesee County View)

No verified named DNR site; official policy is parcel-based — check DNR mineral ownership maps. Ropes Mine (Ishpeming) and most UP gold range ground is private/corporate.

Rules: Hand panning on qualifying state land: no permit, ½ oz/yr cap. Hand sluice requires EGLE Part 301 general permit ($50), Jul 1–Aug 31 only, max 52x12x6.5 in, 300 sq ft area. No power sluices or dredges. State parks: no panning.
Check first: https://www.michigan.gov/dnr/managing-resources/minerals/recreational-gold-panning-and-sluicing-on-state-land

## Wisconsin

- **Nugget Lake County Park – Plum Creek** (Plum City, Pierce Co.) · Pierce County Parks · Panning openly welcomed in creek bed only, no bank digging; pans loanable at office; park entry ~$5/day (715-639-5611) · https://www.mindat.org/loc-25689.html (site history; confirm fees with park)
- **Chequamegon-Nicolet National Forest streams** (Medford / Taylor Co., Black River headwaters) · USDA Forest Service · No permit; pan, small shovel, hand pick only; disturbance <1 sq ft spots, <40 sq ft per 500 ft of stream, <5 days/yr; no sluice, dredge, chemicals; trout waters closed before Apr 15 and after Sep 15; no wilderness/RNA · https://www.fs.usda.gov/r09/chequamegon-nicolet/recreation/opportunities/other

Rules: DNR requires no mining or water permit for non-mechanized panning removing ≤2 cu yd per waterway per year; DNR-owned property may need a collector's permit from the property manager; state parks closed. Public-trust navigable waters can be waded, but minerals belong to the riparian owner — get permission.
Check first: https://dnr.wisconsin.gov/topic/Mines/GoldPanning.html

## Minnesota

- **Zumbro River near Oronoco / Zumbro Falls** (Olmsted–Wabasha Co.) · State-owned riverbed, public water accesses · Hand pan and hand shovel only; wading-level impact; no permit · secondary (mindat/goldrushnuggets) — not an officially designated site
- **Vermilion River near Orr; Little Fork River near Cook** (St. Louis Co.) · State-owned beds, state forest frontage · Hand tools only; avoid designated trout streams without contacting Area Fisheries · secondary — not officially designated

No verified officially designated site; DNR publishes rules, not locations. No pay-to-pan gold sites found.

Rules: No permit for hand panning in state-owned beds of streams, rivers and lakes if impact is no greater than wading. Prohibited in state parks and SNAs (Minn. Rules 6100.0900). Officers can close sites during spawning or crowding. Sluices/dredges count as commercial — require permits and mineral lease.
Check first: https://www.dnr.state.mn.us/geologyrec/index.html

## Indiana

- **Morgan-Monroe State Forest (Gose Creek, Deadman Hollow, Jordan Hollow)** (Martinsville, Morgan Co.) · Indiana DNR Division of Forestry · FREE gold panning permit from forest office (765-342-4026); pan only — no pick, shovel, sluice, or metal detector · https://faqs.in.gov/hc/en-us/articles/115005242428-Is-gold-panning-allowed-on-Indiana-state-forests
- **Yellowwood State Forest** (Nashville, Brown Co.) · Indiana DNR Division of Forestry · FREE permit from office (812-988-7945); pan only, same restrictions · same URL
- **Hoosier National Forest** (Bedford / Tell City) · USDA Forest Service · DNR says a free NF permit is required; hand tools, no motorized digging — call the forest office first · https://www.in.gov/dnr/water/recreational-gold-prospecting

Rules: DNR Division of Water "general license" (312 IAC 10-5-11, 312 IAC 6-5-10) covers panning, non-motorized sluice, pick/shovel, and suction nozzles ≤5 in, sunrise–sunset, no mercury/chemicals, written landowner permission; State Forests require an additional free permit and restrict to pans only. State parks and fish & wildlife areas: no prospecting.
Check first: https://www.in.gov/dnr/water/recreational-gold-prospecting
