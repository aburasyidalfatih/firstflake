# Public Recreational Gold Panning — Western US Research

Compiled 2026-10-06 from agency sources (BLM, USFS, state DNR / geological surveys / state parks) with DetectorProspector/GPAA as secondary. "Casual use" below means hand pans, non-motorized sluices and hand tools on federal land open to mineral entry and not under an active claim (43 CFR 3809 / 36 CFR 228). Always check claim status in BLM MLRS before digging.

## California

- **South Yuba Recreation Area** (Nevada City) · BLM Mother Lode FO + USFS + CA State Parks · Wild & Scenic South Yuba, hands-and-pans ONLY, no sluices or tools, free · https://www.blm.gov/visit/south-yuba-recreation-area
- **Keysville (Keyesville) SRMA** (Lake Isabella, Kern River) · BLM Bakersfield FO · withdrawn area set aside for recreational mining; pans, non-motorized sluices and dry washers (collection surface ≤6 sq ft); no highbanking, dredging or motors; free, 14-day dispersed camping · https://www.blm.gov/visit/keysville-special-recreation-management-area
- **Forks of Butte Creek Recreation Area** (Magalia / Chico) · BLM Redding FO · mineral-withdrawn, numbered 100-yd sites; pans and hand sluices OK, no motors or highbanking; historically a $5/day site permit — confirm with Redding FO (530-224-2100) · https://www.explorebuttecounty.com/activities/discover-the-forks-of-butte-creek-recreation-area
- **Merced River Recreation Management Area** (Briceburg, Mariposa) · BLM Mother Lode FO · non-motorized panning along the Wild & Scenic Merced incl. Cable Rock day-use; some grandfathered claims, check map at Briceburg Visitor Center; $10/night campgrounds · https://www.blm.gov/visit/merced-river
- **Trinity River, Junction City reach** (Junction City / Weaverville) · BLM Redding FO · pans and small non-motorized gear at BLM access points along Hwy 299; respect active claims; no suction dredging · https://www.blm.gov/visit/junction-city-campground-0
- **Marshall Gold Discovery SHP** (Coloma) and **Auburn SRA** (Auburn) · CA State Parks · hands-and-pans only (gold pan is the only tool allowed, 15 lb/day material limit, no metal detectors); park day-use fee at Marshall · https://www.parks.ca.gov/?page_id=502

Rules: No permit for casual-use panning or non-motorized sluicing on BLM/USFS land open to mineral entry; suction dredging is banned statewide; State Parks are hands-and-pans only; National Parks closed. Wilderness and withdrawn areas have extra limits.
Check first: https://www.blm.gov/visit/south-yuba-recreation-area (and the field office that manages your spot)

## Oregon

- **Little Applegate / Tunnel Ridge** (Ruch, near Medford) · BLM Medford District · withdrawn from mineral entry, pans and hand tools, free · https://traveloregon.com/things-to-do/culture-history/historic-sites-oregon-trail/gold-panning-in-oregon/
- **Gold Nugget Wayside** (Gold Hill, Rogue River) · BLM Medford District · small withdrawn waysides on Hwy 234, panning without claims, free · https://traveloregon.com/things-to-do/culture-history/historic-sites-oregon-trail/gold-panning-in-oregon/
- **Quartzville Recreation Corridor** (Sweet Home) · BLM Northwest Oregon District · recreational mining allowed on Quartzville Creek between Rocky Top Bridge and Galena Creek; equipment rules vary year to year, Yellowbottom site itself excluded · https://www.blm.gov/visit/quartzville-back-country-byway
- **Cow Creek Back Country Byway (Island Creek area)** (Riddle / Roseburg) · BLM Roseburg District · ~1,300 ft withdrawn segment, non-commercial panning and hand sluicing; no facilities, pack it out · https://www.blm.gov/visit/cow-creek-back-country-byway
- **Sixes River Campground** (Port Orford) · BLM Coos Bay District · designated panning area, non-motorized methods within the wetted perimeter only; open Memorial Day weekend to Sept 30; campground fee · https://www.blm.gov/visit/sixes-river-campground

Rules: No federal permit for hand panning. State DSL removal-fill permit not needed for non-motorized work under 1 cu yd per site / 5 cu yd per stream per year in Essential Salmonid Habitat (50 cu yd elsewhere); stay in the wet channel, avoid fish eggs and spawning season. Motorized/suction dredging banned in ESH and needs DEQ 700-PM + DSL authorization elsewhere.
Check first: https://www.oregon.gov/dsl/WW/Pages/Mining.aspx

## Washington

- **Liberty / Swauk Creek** (Liberty, near Cle Elum) · Okanogan-Wenatchee NF, Cle Elum RD · state's most popular panning area; casual use on unclaimed NF ground (many active claims — check MLRS); Gold and Fish pamphlet rules and work windows apply · https://wdfw.wa.gov/sites/default/files/publications/02150/wdfw02150.pdf
- **Sultan River / Skykomish River** (Sultan, Gold Bar) · mixed USFS/DNR/private; Snohomish County · hand pan and small sluice under pamphlet; Spada Lake watershed above Culmback Dam closed · https://wdfw.medium.com/panning-for-gold-in-washington-waters-rules-hot-spots-and-tips-628ded956b7a
- **Snoqualmie River / Tolt River** (North Bend, Carnation) · mixed public access · pamphlet rules; fine gold in gravel bars · https://wdfw.medium.com/panning-for-gold-in-washington-waters-rules-hot-spots-and-tips-628ded956b7a
- **Wenatchee River / Peshastin Creek (Blewett district)** (Leavenworth) · Okanogan-Wenatchee NF · casual use, check claims; pamphlet work windows · https://wdfw.medium.com/panning-for-gold-in-washington-waters-rules-hot-spots-and-tips-628ded956b7a

Rules: Carry the current WDFW "Gold and Fish" pamphlet — it is your free Hydraulic Project Approval for pans, mini-rocker boxes and small non-motorized sluices, with river-by-river in-water work windows to protect fish. Any motorized method (dredge, highbanker) needs a written HPA plus an Ecology NPDES permit. State Parks are closed to panning; DNR aquatic lands may need authorization.
Check first: https://wdfw.wa.gov/sites/default/files/publications/02150/wdfw02150.pdf

## Idaho

- **South Fork Payette River, Sawtooth Wilderness boundary to Banks** (Lowman / Garden Valley / Banks) · Idaho Dept of Lands (riverbed) · state-designated public recreational mining section, closed to new claims; panning free, suction dredging needs the IDWR Letter Permit; steep canyon, expert water · https://www.idl.idaho.gov/mining-minerals/dredge-placer-mining/dredge-and-placer-mining-frequently-asked-questions/
- **Boise Basin — Idaho City area creeks (e.g., Bannock Creek)** (Idaho City) · Boise NF, Idaho City RD (208-392-3700) · casual-use panning on unclaimed NF ground; heavy claim coverage, verify first · https://www.idl.idaho.gov/mining-minerals/dredge-placer-mining/recreational-mining/
- **Salmon-Challis NF** (Salmon / Challis) · USFS · no designated panning area; casual panning allowed on open, unclaimed ground per forest guide · https://www.fs.usda.gov/media/112818

Rules: Pans and hand tools need no permit on open BLM/USFS/state riverbeds without claims. Anything that moves material with a sluice fed by a pump, or a suction dredge (≤5 in intake, ≤15 hp), needs the annual IDWR Small Scale Mining Letter Permit ($10 resident / $30 non-resident) with open-stream list and seasons; some waters (e.g., South Fork Boise below Anderson Ranch, North Fork/main Payette near Banks) are closed.
Check first: https://idwr.idaho.gov/form/stream/

## Montana

- **Libby Creek Recreational Gold Panning Area** (Libby, 23 mi S) · Kootenai NF, Libby RD (406-293-7773) · withdrawn 30-acre area, pans/picks/shovels/buckets only — no sluices, rockers, motors, wheelbarrows or metal detectors; free, primitive camping, vault toilet · https://visitmt.com/listing/libby-creek-gold-panning-area-6952
- **Bannack State Park** (Dillon) · Montana State Parks · supervised weekend panning tubs in summer only (not free-range prospecting); park entry fee · https://visitmt.com/things-to-do/gold-and-gem-mining
- **Garnet Ghost Town** (Missoula, 1 hr E) · BLM Missoula FO · panning offered only during annual Garnet Day event; $10 entry · https://www.garnetghosttown.org/things-to-do.php
- **Beaverhead-Deerlodge NF (Virginia City / Butte area creeks)** (Virginia City, Philipsburg) · USFS · casual panning on unclaimed ground; contact ranger district; county 310 permit may be required for in-stream disturbance · https://fs.usda.gov/activity/bdnf/recreation/rocks-minerals

Rules: USFS Northern Region and BLM Montana need no permit for gold panning or hand tools that cause negligible disturbance. Sluicing/dredging requires a 310 permit from the county conservation district, DEQ general permit for suction dredges (<4 in intake), and a Notice of Intent to the Forest. State trust land needs a Recreational Use License; state parks closed unless designated.
Check first: https://www.fs.usda.gov/r01/natural-resources/geology/recreational-prospecting-montana

## Nevada

- **Rye Patch / Majuba placers** (Lovelock, ~22-45 mi N; west of Rye Patch Reservoir) · BLM Humboldt River FO (Winnemucca District) · famous chevron-nugget metal-detecting ground; hand tools, pans, detectors and drywashers on open BLM parcels; checkerboard with private railroad land and claims; no collecting inside Rye Patch SRA (state park) · https://www.blm.gov/documents/nevada/public-room/brochure/collecting-public-lands
- **Sawtooth Knob / Placerites / Rabbit Hole districts** (Lovelock / Winnemucca, Pershing-Humboldt counties) · BLM · shallow placers suited to drywasher and detector; verify claims in MLRS · https://pubs.nbmg.unr.edu/Recreational_gold_prospecting_p/gg12.htm
- **Unionville / Buena Vista Canyon** (Unionville, Pershing County) · BLM · historic placers, hand-tool casual use on unclaimed ground · https://nbmg.unr.edu/GeneralGeology/Rocks&Minerals.html

Rules: BLM Nevada "Collecting on Public Lands": gold and silver may be prospected with hand tools, pans and metal detectors without a permit on land open to prospecting; non-mechanical panning is even allowed in wilderness/WSAs if no surface disturbance. Sluicing, dredging or any mechanical equipment needs permits (contact NDOW for dredges; NDEP water permits). Nevada has 180,000+ active claims — claim checks are mandatory. No formally designated free panning parks.
Check first: https://www.blm.gov/documents/nevada/public-room/brochure/collecting-public-lands

## Arizona

- **Lynx Creek Gold Pan Day Use Area** (Prescott, off Walker Rd) · Prescott NF, Bradshaw RD · withdrawn from mineral entry; pans and metal detectors; no sluices, dredges or drywashers; free; seasonal day-use hours, vault toilet, no water · https://www.fs.usda.gov/r03/prescott/recreation/lynx-creek-gold-pan-day-use-area
- **Hassayampa River / Bradshaw Mountains BLM ground** (Wickenburg) · BLM Hassayampa FO · casual use (pans, hand drywashers <10 hp, detectors) on open parcels around heavily claimed blocks · https://www.fs.usda.gov/r03/prescott/recreation/rocks-minerals
- **Gold Basin district** (Kingman, N) · BLM Kingman FO · popular nugget-detecting area; hand tools and drywashers on unclaimed BLM land · https://www.blm.gov/sites/default/files/documents/files/collecting_on_publiclands.pdf

Rules: Low-impact panning, hand collecting and metal detecting need no Notice of Intent on USFS; BLM casual use is permit-free. State Trust Land is closed to prospecting without a permit. Lynx Creek is the only free, designated USFS panning area in the state.
Check first: https://www.fs.usda.gov/r03/prescott/recreation/rocks-minerals

## Colorado

- **Cache Creek Placer Area** (Granite, between Leadville and Buena Vista) · BLM Royal Gorge FO (719-269-8500) · not claimable; panning and non-motorized sluicing in a designated dig zone; motorized access Memorial Day to Nov 30 (elk closure); BLM-approved fee $5/day or $25/yr — confirm enforcement · https://www.blm.gov/royal-gorge-fo/public-room/map/blm-co-rgfo-cache-creek-placer-area-map
- **Point Barr Placer Area** (Salida, 8 mi S on US 50) · BLM / CPW Arkansas Headwaters Rec Area · 37-acre public placer; hand panning free (AHRA day fee); motorized gear needs a $30/device/season permit from AHRA Visitor Center, Apr 1 to Sep 30, 4 in nozzle/8 hp limits; 4WD advised · https://cpw.state.co.us/state-parks/arkansas-headwaters-recreation-area/arkansas-headwaters-recreation-area-salida-vallie
- **Arapahoe Bar, Clear Creek** (Wheat Ridge, W of Youngfield St) · City of Wheat Ridge Parks (303-205-7550) · panning and sluicing in creek bed only between marked signs; battery gear in a box OK, no gas motors; free · https://www.rootedinfun.com/facilities/facility/details/Clear-Creek-Trail-5
- **Clear Creek Canyon Park** (Golden, US 6) · Jefferson County Open Space · ~10 mi of creek; panning, sluicing, dredges ≤4 in; fill holes, no digging within 100 ft of structures; free · https://www.jeffco.us/1196/Clear-Creek-Canyon-Park
- **Fairplay Beach / Middle Fork South Platte** (Fairplay) · Town of Fairplay · pan/sluice downstream of the posted sign to the US 285 bridge; small permit booked through Town Hall · https://www.uncovercolorado.com/activities/fairplay-beach/

Rules: Casual-use panning on BLM/USFS is permit-free, but Colorado's best beginner spots are managed parcels with their own posted rules; motorized or pumped equipment on the Arkansas needs the BLM/CPW placer notification and reclamation bond and is closed Oct 1 to Mar 31 for trout spawning.
Check first: https://www.blm.gov/royal-gorge-fo/public-room/map/blm-co-rgfo-cache-creek-placer-area-map

## Alaska

- **Nome Creek Valley, White Mountains NRA** (Fairbanks, Steese Hwy MP 57) · BLM Eastern Interior FO (907-474-2200) · 4-mile stretch between Nome Creek Bridge and Moose Creek; pans, rockers, hand sluices, picks/shovels; no suction dredges; season May 1 to Oct 14; road construction closures 2026 · https://www.blm.gov/visit/white-mountains
- **Petersville Recreational Mining Area** (Trapper Creek, Petersville Rd) · Alaska DNR Div. of Mining, Land & Water · 274-acre state area along Peters Creek closed to new claims; pan/pick/shovel free; dredges ≤18 hp need ADF&G + DEC permits, May 15 to Jul 15 · https://dnr.alaska.gov/mlw/cdn/pdf/factsheets/petersville-recreational-mining-area.pdf
- **Caribou Creek Recreational Mining Area** (Glacier View, Glenn Hwy MP 104) · Alaska DNR · active floodplain of Caribou Creek; closed to mineral entry; general ADF&G permit with seasonal fish restrictions; steep foot trail, free · https://dnr.alaska.gov/mlw/cdn/pdf/factsheets/caribou-creek-recreational-mining-area.pdf
- **Hatcher Pass Public Use Area / Little Susitna River** (Palmer, 15 mi N) · Alaska DNR + State Parks · pan/pick/shovel anywhere except active claims; dredge ≤6 in/18 hp; Little Su is anadromous so ADF&G permit may be needed; Independence Mine SHP shovels-and-pans only · https://dnr.alaska.gov/mlw/cdn/pdf/factsheets/hatcher-pass-public-use-area.pdf
- **Resurrection Creek** (Hope, Kenai Peninsula) · Chugach NF, Seward RD · 0.5-mile withdrawal upstream of Resurrection Pass Trail footbridge; pans and hand tools free; 4 in dredges May 15 to Jul 15 with ADF&G + ADEC permits; also Bertha, Sixmile and Crescent Creek panning areas · https://www.detectorprospector.com/magazine/gold-prospecting/alaska-resurrection-creek-gold-panning-area/
- **Nome East and West Public Beach Mining Areas** (Nome) · Alaska DNR · free state beach areas, open-water season roughly June to Sept; no winter under-ice dredging · https://dnr.alaska.gov/mlw/factsheets/

Rules: On BLM and state land hand tools, pans, rockers and hand sluices are permit-free; motorized equipment needs ADF&G fish-habitat and DEC permits and is season-limited on salmon streams. Private, Native-corporation and claimed ground is trespass without consent.
Check first: https://www.blm.gov/programs/recreation/recreation-activities/alaska

## Wyoming

- **South Pass City State Historic Site** (Lander / Atlantic City, Hwy 28) · Wyoming State Parks · teaching panning with staff and $5 guaranteed-flake bags; no collecting inside site boundary · https://windriver.org/experience/gold-panning-ghost-towns/
- **Rock Creek / Atlantic City area BLM** (Atlantic City) · BLM Lander FO · panning for personal use allowed outside active claims (many claims — check MLRS); hand tools only · https://experience.arcgis.com/experience/cbaa2b72d3c64982bcd08136939194ba
- **Douglas Creek district** (Fox Park / Laramie) · Medicine Bow-Routt NF, Laramie RD · popular amateur placer area; in-stream work season reported as Jul 1 to Sep 10 for trout — confirm with district · https://main.wsgs.wyo.gov/mineral-resources/metals/mining-districts/medicine-bow-mountains

Rules: BLM Wyoming: personal-use panning is allowed on BLM land outside existing claims with no permit or charge. State DEQ/LQD does not regulate recreational panning with hand tools; dredges >3 in intake or >10 hp, or motorized trenching, need LQD permits. Checkerboard ownership — verify land status.
Check first: https://www.blm.gov/sites/blm.gov/files/documents/files/WYNF-0004_rockhounding%20(051418).pdf

## Utah

- **American Fork Canyon (above and below Tibble Fork Reservoir)** (American Fork / Pleasant Grove) · Uinta-Wasatch-Cache NF, Pleasant Grove RD · most consistent recreational panning in the state; pans and non-mechanized gear on unclaimed ground; canyon recreation fee ($10/3 days) · https://geology.utah.gov/popular/general-geology/rocks-and-minerals/utah-gold/recreational-gold-panning-and-dredging-regulations/
- **Crescent Creek, Henry Mountains** (Hanksville, 25 mi S) · BLM · fine placer gold; casual use hand tools; verify claims · https://geology.utah.gov/popular/general-geology/rocks-and-minerals/utah-gold/recreational-gold-panning-and-dredging-regulations/
- **Johnson and Recapture Creeks, Abajo Mountains** (Monticello / Blanding) · Manti-La Sal NF / BLM · fine placer, hand panning · https://geology.utah.gov/popular/general-geology/rocks-and-minerals/utah-gold/recreational-gold-panning-and-dredging-regulations/

Rules: Non-mechanized panning needs no state permit on BLM/USFS land without claims. Any suction dredge (≤4 in, ≤12 hp) or hand sluice work in a stream channel needs the free annual Recreational Dredging permit from the Division of Water Rights (reissued each April, 45 days/yr, open-stream list) plus prior notice to the land agency; Green, Colorado and San Juan rivers are closed. SITLA trust lands need a lease.
Check first: https://www.waterrights.utah.gov/strmalt/default.asp

## New Mexico

- **Hillsboro district placers (Animas Hills, Dutch Gulch, Rio Percha)** (Hillsboro, Sierra County) · BLM Las Cruces District + private · state's richest placer district; casual-use hand tools/drywashers on unclaimed BLM parcels; much of Percha Creek is private · https://geoinfo.nmt.edu/resources/minerals/metallic/gold/placer/home.html
- **Pinos Altos / Bear Creek** (Pinos Altos, near Silver City) · Gila NF, Silver City RD · casual panning on open NF ground; heavily claimed, verify MLRS · https://geoinfo.nmt.edu/resources/minerals/metallic/gold/placer/home.html
- **Jicarilla district** (Lincoln County, near White Oaks) · Lincoln NF, Smokey Bear RD · black-sand dry placers still worked recreationally; drywasher country · https://geoinfo.nmt.edu/resources/minerals/metallic/gold/placer/home.html
- **Elizabethtown-Baldy / Moreno Valley** (Eagle Nest) · mostly private (Philmont etc.) · historic; limited public access, listed for context only · https://geoinfo.nmt.edu/resources/minerals/metallic/gold/placer/home.html

Rules: No formally designated public panning parks. Hand panning, non-motorized sluicing, drywashing and detecting are casual use on BLM/USFS land open to mineral entry (no permit); state trust land needs State Land Office permission; tribal land is closed. Arid — expect drywashing. Check claims; many districts are blanketed.
Check first: https://geoinfo.nmt.edu/resources/minerals/metallic/gold/placer/home.html

## South Dakota

- **French Creek, Custer State Park** (Custer) · SD Game, Fish & Parks · hand panning in designated areas along the 1874 discovery creek; no sluices, digging banks or motors; park entrance fee · https://gfp.sd.gov/parks/detail/custer-state-park/
- **Rapid Creek / Castle Creek near Rochford** (Rochford / Hill City) · Black Hills NF, Mystic RD · casual panning and hand sluicing on unclaimed NF ground; many claims incl. GPAA club claims · https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/blackhills/recreation/gold-panning-rockhounding-metal-detecting
- **Spring Creek / Battle Creek drainages** (Hill City / Keystone) · Black Hills NF · same casual-use rules; fine flour gold · https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/blackhills/recreation/gold-panning-rockhounding-metal-detecting

Rules: Black Hills NF allows panning and sluicing with hand tools without authorization but has no designated panning sites; closed in wilderness, acquired-mineral lands and withdrawals. South Dakota exempts hand-tool recreational mining (pans, sluices, detectors) from state permits; a DANR mine permit is required for any dredge or mechanized gear. Private inholdings and claims are common — use the forest map.
Check first: https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/blackhills/recreation/gold-panning-rockhounding-metal-detecting
